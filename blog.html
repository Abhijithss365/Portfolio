My Career in Cybersecurity

I am building my career in cybersecurity with a focus on ethical hacking, penetration testing, network security, and practical security work. My goal is to understand how systems and networks work, identify security weaknesses, and develop the skills needed to help protect them.

I am developing my knowledge through hands-on projects, Linux practice, security labs, networking concepts, and continuous learning. I also want to improve my problem-solving and communication skills as I progress toward a professional cybersecurity role.

Cybersecurity Skills

My current skill set includes ethical hacking fundamentals, network security, OSINT, security-focused Linux usage, and basic web security. These areas help me understand common security concepts and approach practical cybersecurity problems step by step.
